## Auditus

通过可视化 UI 感知游戏原本通过声音传达的事件、方向、节奏和氛围。

| 方向 | 事件 | 节奏 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 周边图标随视角变化，呈现声音的粗略方位 | 字幕与图形区分生物活动、交流、物品和环境声音 | 通过动态表现与白、黄、红警示传达声音变化 |

支持**仅字幕、仅提示、字幕与提示**三种模式。

## Visus · 未来支持

通过声音指示感知游戏原本通过视觉传达的事件、方向、距离、状态和环境变化。

## 使用

依赖 **BepInEx 5**。将 `Sensus.dll` 放入 `BepInEx/plugins/Auuueser-Sensus/`。

支持 **LethalConfig** 局内设置与中英双语。检测到 [LC Chinese Project](https://github.com/Auuueser/LC-Chinese-Project) 时自动使用中文，并将音频对白字幕交由汉化管理。

### Audiovisual accessibility & environmental awareness

### Auditus

Visualize the events, direction, rhythm and atmosphere originally conveyed through sound.

Directional icons, event captions and visual rhythm cues cover supported creature, player, item and environmental sounds. Choose captions, indicators, or both.

### Visus · Planned

Use audio cues to convey events, direction, distance, state and environmental changes originally communicated visually.

### Setup

Requires **BepInEx 5**. Place `Sensus.dll` in `BepInEx/plugins/Auuueser-Sensus/`.

Optional **LethalConfig** integration provides in-game settings. Chinese and English are supported. When **LC Chinese Project** is detected, Chinese is selected automatically and that mod manages spoken dialogue subtitles.
